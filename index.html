Dim to warm

Ordinary dimming makes a room darker but leaves the light cold. Dim to warm follows a candle instead. As the brightness drops the colour falls with it, from a crisp 3000K white down through 2700K and 2200K to an 1800K amber, until the room is carried almost entirely by the fittings themselves.

A family that has been selling light since the seventies

Shwash Crystal was built by Abdus Sattar, who started shaping Bangladesh's lighting trade in the 1970s, long before LEDs arrived. His daughter Israt joined in the early 2000s and now runs purchasing, marketing and sales, keeping the standard her father set while pulling the business toward how people shop today.
